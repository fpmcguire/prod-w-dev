# Product Owner Sign-off: STEP-07

## Recommendation

I recommend **REQUEST_REAL_HUMAN_PILOT**.

The simulated STEP-07 pilot is useful and reviewable. It is sufficient as narrow evidence that PROD-W's record forms and protocol concepts can be followed into a coherent, traceable, visibly non-progressing record in one bounded scripted case. It is **not** sufficient, from the intended product-team perspective, to treat AC-3 as satisfied for user applicability.

Before PROD-W claims AC-3 evidence for real product-team use, I require a later pilot with real human actors performing the consequential product-role acts. That later pilot does not need to reopen the current record-shape finding, but it must test the parts this pilot could only label, script, count, or scenario-walk.

This sign-off does not record final STEP-07 acceptance. Final acceptance remains MOD-W Moderator-owned.

## Decision on Pilot Sufficiency

The current pilot is acceptable only as a **simulated record-shape / protocol-feasibility pilot**. It shows that:

- the method can produce a complete-looking record set for one bounded opportunity;
- invalid self-approval, authority gaps, open requirements, formal-check limits, and gate refusal can be made visible;
- the STEP-06 methodology can be followed without changing accepted product artifacts;
- issue routing and conservative findings can preserve unresolved questions.

The current pilot is not acceptable as evidence that intended product teams can or will use PROD-W successfully. One AI session wrote every actor. No human performed a recorded act. Gate outcomes and five events were scripted. The record therefore cannot test real independence, real disagreement, actor-kind binding, human burden, or whether people understand the authority limits before they matter.

Product Owner decision: **a later real-human pilot is required before AC-3 is treated as satisfied for user applicability.**

## Blocking Items

### PO7-B1: AC-3 user-applicability evidence is not yet satisfied

The current pilot supports only the narrow record-shape/protocol-feasibility claim. It does not satisfy AC-3 from the intended product-team perspective if AC-3 is read to include real user applicability.

Required before that stronger claim:

- at least two real human actors, and preferably the STEP-06 focus case of three humans plus an agent, must perform the consequential acts;
- product-role acts must not be merely scripted by one author;
- the pilot must measure human time and burden;
- independence declarations, challenge behavior, gate readiness use, self-approval prevention, and disagreement/non-progression must be exercised by real people.

### PO7-B2: Human burden remains untested

The 58-record, approximately 14,000-word record set is a strong warning signal, not a human usability result. Counts and AI elapsed time cannot answer whether a product team can complete the minimum artifact set in a normal work cadence.

### PO7-B3: Small-team authority usability remains unproven

The pilot shows the record can say "stop" when a solo or two-human team cannot validly accept. It does not show that a real solo founder or two-human team will understand that before writing misleading grants, supplying wording, or relying on a conflicted reviewer.
